Privacy Policy - Schengen Rule Tracker

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview & Privacy-First Architecture

"Schengen Rule Tracker" is built with a strict privacy-first, on-device architecture. We do not operate user accounts, remote databases, or cloud analytics services.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

All data entered into the application (such as trip dates, destination countries, traveler profile names, and visa parameters) is stored exclusively on your device's local memory (AsyncStorage sandbox). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

3. Advertisements

The application displays non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob. In compliance with privacy regulations and Apple guidelines, non-personalized advertising is strictly enforced (`requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true`). No cross-app tracking or user profiling is conducted.

4. Third-Party Access

Because all core user data is stored strictly on your local device, no third party has access to your trip history, passport details, or personal information.
