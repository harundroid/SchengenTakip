❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Do I need an account or login to use the app?
No account or login is required. The app is completely accessible immediately upon download with zero registration barriers.
2. Where and how is my data saved?
All trip entries, traveler profiles, and visa configurations are saved 100% locally on your device using native local storage (AsyncStorage). We do not operate external cloud database servers or track user data.
3. How do I back up my trip data?
Because data is stored securely in your device's local app sandbox, it is automatically included in standard iOS device backups (via iCloud or Mac/Finder backup). Restoring an iOS backup will restore your full trip history.
4. How is the 90/180-day rolling window calculated?
Calculations strictly follow official European Union Regulation (EU) 2016/399 (Schengen Borders Code). On any given date of your stay, the app looks back exactly 180 days and calculates the total cumulative days spent across all Schengen member states.
5. Can I track stays in countries outside Schengen?
Yes! Use the zone selector bar at the top to add independent non-Schengen destinations (such as North Macedonia, Kosovo, Turkey, United Kingdom, etc.) to calculate single-country visa stay limits.
